# TaleLah — 30-second UGC video

**Format** 9:16 vertical · **Length** 30s hard cap · **Tone** handheld and authentic, not a polished ad
**Deliverable** one social cut for LinkedIn / X, tagged `@QoderOfficial` `@AlibabaCloud` `#QoderHackathon` `#BuildWithQoder`

---

## The spine

The three seeded stories share one target phrase, in three languages:

| Story | Language | Target phrase | Means |
|---|---|---|---|
| 小猫回家 | Chinese | **谢谢你** | thank you |
| அன்பு யானை | Tamil | **உதவிக்கு நன்றி** | thank you for the help |
| Kancil yang Lapar | Malay | **Terima kasih** | thank you |

**Three children, three languages, one word — each said to a real person in the room.**
Everything in the 30 seconds builds to that.

## One hard rule

Every **app screen is a real recording** from `app.talelah.com`.
Only the family b-roll is generated or filmed. A judge can tell the difference.

## Music

Soft piano or kora. Warm, no lyrics. Gentle build through scenes 3–7, resolve on the
end card. **Drop to silence for half a second right before the boy runs** — the pause
is what sells scene 8.

---

## Three things that make or break it

**1 · Scene 8 is the product.** It is the only shot that shows what TaleLah is *for* —
a child leaving the screen to speak to a person. If you must cut for time, take Marina
Bay down to 2 seconds. Never cut scene 8.

**2 · Do not show the loading.** Generation takes roughly 15–60 seconds. Cut straight
from the tap to the story card. Every product video does this and nobody minds.

**3 · Do not caption the AI shots as real families.** Scenes 2 and 8 use generated
actors. Keep "real families" language for `evidence/pilot/`, where it is actually true.
Overclaiming here is the one thing that could cost more than it gains.

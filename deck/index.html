Why another lessons app can't fix it

School gives lessons. Only home gives use.

More drills is more of exactly what already isn't working. A language survives when a child has a reason to speak it after school.

The architecture

Six agents, one pipeline.

1Moment LensReads the photo or note.Qwen-VL
2Learning PlannerPicks one speaking goal.Qwen
3Book AuthorDesigns the whole book.per-language
4Language GuardianChecks every native line./specs/lang
5Family VoiceNarrates & hands off.CosyVoice·Sarvam
6Growth CoachRemembers, never scores.Qwen

A safety gate sits between the pipeline and the child — nothing reaches child mode without passing it and the parent.

# Consent — read this to the parent before starting

Copy this into each session file and tick the boxes with the parent present.

---

## What we are asking

We would like to watch your child use TaleLah for about 20 minutes, and write
down what happens. We are testing **our app**, never your child. There is no
score, no result, and nothing to pass or fail.

## What we record

- **Written notes only** — where your child paused, what they said in their
  own language, what you thought of it.
- Your comments, which we may quote **without your name or your child's name**.

## What we do not record

- **No photographs or video of your child.** No faces, ever.
- **No recordings of your child's voice.** The app turns speech into an intent
  on the server and discards the audio; we keep nothing.
- **No real names.** Your child appears in our notes as "Child A / B / C".

## Your control

- You can stop at any moment, for any reason, without explaining.
- You can ask us to delete the notes afterwards and we will, no questions.
- Any story created stays in your account. You can delete it in the app.

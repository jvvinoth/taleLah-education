## Honest limits of this pilot

- Three families is a signal, not evidence of retention.
- Sessions were observed by the team, which flatters the result.
- Every child was in a home already motivated about mother-tongue use.
- One session per family — we have not tested whether it survives day two.

**What we would need next:** a two-week run with ten families, unobserved,
measuring the north-star metric — *completed family language moments per
active child per week.*
